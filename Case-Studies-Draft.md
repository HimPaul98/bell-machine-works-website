## Semiconductor Equipment
*BELL's primary stated industry — this is the strongest-matching real evidence in the whole dataset.*

### ASML — Lithography System Brackets & Vacuum Seal Components
**Sector:** Semiconductor Equipment – Lithography (EUV/DUV) · **Material:** Aluminum 6061-T6

BELL has machined critical alignment brackets and vacuum/gas sealing components for ASML's lithography systems — the machines that print the circuitry on nearly every advanced chip made today. Two separate engagements: a 3F alignment bracket holding precise tube alignment through the optical path, paired with a "popless" fusing lid designed to prevent particle contamination in clean-room environments; and an O-ring sealed bracket and plate set with six tight-tolerance sealing locations (down to 0.079" plate thickness) maintaining vacuum integrity for wafer processing. This is exactly the kind of work where a tolerance miss doesn't mean a bad part — it means particle contamination in an EUV chamber. 📸

### Texas Institute for Electronics (UT Austin) — Copper Baseplate for Semiconductor R&D
**Sector:** Academic Research – Semiconductor R&D · **Material:** Copper C110

A large-format high-conductivity copper baseplate for thermal management in advanced packaging research — supporting 3D heterogeneous integration and chiplet R&D at UT Austin's advanced packaging lab. Proof point that BELL's semiconductor-equipment work extends from production-line hardware (ASML) to the R&D bench driving next-generation chip packaging.

## Aerospace Components
*Four distinct real engagements, spanning launch vehicles, satellites, and certified test labs.*

### Stoke Space — Tube Raceway Brackets for Reusable Launch Vehicle Propulsion
**Sector:** Aerospace – Reusable Rocket Manufacturing · **Material:** Delrin 150 (white) and PTFE (black, cryo-compatible variant)

BELL machined two related bracket families for Stoke Space's Nova program — a fully reusable medium-lift launch vehicle under development at Stoke's Kent, WA facility. The first: white Delrin top/bottom raceway brackets organizing parallel tube and fluid lines with contoured saddles and structural ribs to prevent deflection under vibration. The second: a six-part black PTFE clamp family (standard, "special," and cryo-inert "Y-Inert" variants, 90 pieces total) selected specifically for cryogenic compatibility and chemical inertness — consistent with propellant and inerting line management on actual flight hardware. Both shipped on expedited next-day timelines supporting active propulsion system integration. 📸

### SpaceX — Cable Clamps for Starlink Satellite Production
**Sector:** Aerospace / Satellite Internet Infrastructure · **Material:** Polycarbonate, black

Mirrored left/right cable clamps machined for SpaceX's Woodinville, WA Starlink production facility, shipped with VQC inspection on next-day air for active satellite-line support. Polycarbonate was selected for impact resistance, thermal stability, and UV resistance appropriate to space-rated hardware — precision left/right symmetry was required for correct paired installation on the line.

### Wichita State University — NIAR Environmental Test Labs — DO-160 Test Fixture Components
**Sector:** Aerospace Testing & Certification · **Material:** UHMW PE

A 10-piece UHMW PE fixture set for NIAR's Environmental Test Lab, supporting DO-160 environmental certification testing for airborne equipment — lightning testing, HIRF, temperature/altitude cycling, humidity, and electromagnetic effects testing, across a −40°C to +85°C range. NIAR's client list includes Boeing, Bombardier, Cessna, and Spirit AeroSystems, making this a credible, if indirect, connection to major OEM certification programs.

### Air Concepts International (ACI) — Aircraft Collar Assembly Set
**Sector:** Aviation / Aircraft Interior Parts · **Material:** Aluminum 2024

A full aerospace-grade collar assembly set (left/right bottom collars plus top collars forming 10 complete 4-piece clamping assemblies) for aircraft interior mounting — cabin furniture, seat tracks, and avionics/IFE equipment. This is a direct real-world match to the "collar/clamping assemblies" example already listed on BELL's current homepage — this is the actual job behind that line.

### UCSF Biomedical Engineering — Microfluidic Manifold Halves (Organ-on-Chip Research)
**Sector:** Academic Research / Biomedical Engineering · **Material:** Polycarbonate, clear, bead-blast finish

Two complementary polycarbonate manifold halves for an organ-on-chip microfluidics research device at UCSF's Byers Hall, Mission Bay campus — with a 0.4mm micro-channel width requirement demanding genuine micro-machining precision. This is BELL's strongest available evidence of sub-millimeter feature machining for a demanding academic research client — worth a dedicated micro-features callout wherever BELL discusses its "< Ø 0.01\" micro features" claim.
